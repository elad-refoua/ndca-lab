# מבט כפול · NDCA Lab (prototype)

**Site:** https://elad-refoua.github.io/ndca-lab/

An interactive prototype of a nine-step dialectical-constructivist method of qualitative analysis, the method of
**Prof. Guy Enosh and Tal Eliosef**, carried out step by step by nine AI agents. Each agent does one step of the method
and sees only what that step allows; the researcher decides at two checkpoints, and code checks every run.

## What this is, and what it is not

- **A prototype for demonstration.** It is not a research tool, and its output is not a finding about anyone.
- **Invented data only.** The five interviews in the demo are invented for this prototype. No research participant's
  words are in this repository.
- **What the page shows** is a recorded run of the nine agents on those invented interviews. It can also run the agents
  live on a free, basic AI model through a separate server; what is typed there goes to Google's model, which may use
  it, so real interviews and identifying details must never be entered.
- **The method belongs to its authors.** Their paper is not yet published; this site describes the method in its own
  words and does not reproduce the paper. For the method itself, contact the authors.
